A machine by Travers MacMaster

BOTTOMFEEDER

BOTTOMFEEDER is a phase-distortion and wavefold drum instrument with eight voices, synthesis controls, an effects and LFO rack, and a master glitch chain.

Inside the instrument

What it does

  1. 01Eight individually editable drum voices
  2. 02Pitch, envelopes, phase distortion and wavefold controls
  3. 03Per-voice and master effects with LFO modulation
  4. 04Master Glitch Echo, Repeater and Tape Stop
  5. 05A PHASE and B FOLD modulation sequences
  6. 06Save, load and factory kit controls
