A machine by Travers MacMaster

3FIELD

3FIELD is a 3D host for KaleidoField, MoiréField, SpiroField and Suminagashi. Layer these 2D visual systems on planes, cylinders and spheres, adjust transforms and movement, explore the scene with the camera, and export images or video.
