STRETCH

0.3.0 · Windows VST3

Reshape live audio with stretch, pitch, freeze and reverse controls.

Download Windows VST3 ↓Download SHA-256 checksum ↓

Try it out

Blend the processed sound with MIX, explore captured audio with STRETCH and PITCH, or add movement with the LFO section.

Getting started

Extract the ZIP and read QUICK START - Stretch.txt. Copy the complete Stretch.vst3 folder into your Windows VST3 folder, then rescan plugins. Load it as an effect on a track containing audio or an instrument.
