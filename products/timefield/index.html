A machine by Travers MacMaster

TimeField

TimeField records incoming audio into a four-bar field and reshapes it through six playback heads, a field loop and effects. The PLAY, MODULATION and MIX / FX workspaces organize recording, sequencing, modulation and mixing.

This instrument is not currently available to open on the web.

All machines
  1. 01Six heads with Repeat, Ping-pong, Scan and Hold playback
  2. 02A and B recordings with equal-power blending
  3. 03Manual recording or bar-aligned takes of 1, 2 or 4 bars
  4. 04Sequences of 1, 2 or 4 bars at 1/16 or 1/32 resolution
  5. 05Two LFOs per head across all six heads
  6. 06Per-head Stutter and Head FX
  7. 07Master saturation, bitcrush, delay and reverb
  8. 08Four Scenes with Immediate, Beat or Bar recall
  9. 09Windows x64 VST3 audio effect; MIDI optional
