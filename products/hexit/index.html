A machine by Travers MacMaster

HEXIT

HEXIT is a six-voice sample instrument with sample switching, per-voice sample and effect sequences, a shared six-slot FX bank, and master effects with LFO modulation.

This instrument is not currently available to open on the web.

Getting started

What it does

  1. 01Six sample voices: Kick, Hihat, Snare, Hand, Mallet and Metallic
  2. 02Per-voice sample selection, pitch, envelopes, filter, pan and sample boundaries
  3. 03Independent sample and FX slot sequences
  4. 04Six shared FX slots with per-hit settings
  5. 05Compressor, bitcrusher, wavefolder, ring modulation, filter, comb filter and delay views
  6. 06Two master effects with independent LFO controls
  7. 07Preset and FX bank save/load controls
