A machine by Travers MacMaster

Video Mosaic

Video Mosaic is experimental video art software for creating a larger visual composition from individually moving video clips, arranged as animated tiles inside a painted region of a photograph.

Getting started

What it does

  1. 01Paint the part of a photograph that becomes a moving mosaic
  2. 02Build the mosaic from multiple imported video clips
  3. 03Match video tiles by target color and luminance
  4. 04Control tile size, loop length, seed and animated tile-size sweeps
  5. 05Save projects and export PNG frames, MP4 or WebM recordings
  6. 06Process imported media locally in the browser
