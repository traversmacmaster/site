What you can make

Animated Harmonograph Art Generator

Produce layered harmonograph art ranging from open pendulum drawings to intricate mathematical line fields, with independent pen and canvas controls.

How the process works

Four damped sine pendulums drive a resolution-independent drawing trajectory. Frequency, phase, amplitude and damping shape the motion while the on-screen mechanism reflects the same oscillator values.

Inputs and source material

Start from a frequency relationship or preset, then tune the four oscillators and drawing controls directly.

Save and export

Commit drawings into layers, save the project and export the finished composition as genuine vector SVG or transparent-capable PNG.

Getting started

How to use Harmano

  1. 01Choose a frequency relationship or adjust the oscillators directly.
  2. 02Run the drawing and change phase, damping and pen settings to shape the curve.
  3. 03Commit drawings to layers, choose the canvas size and export the composition as SVG or PNG.
