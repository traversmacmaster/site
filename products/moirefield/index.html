MoiréFieldmoiré pattern generator

MoiréField is a moiré pattern generator by Travers MacMaster. Layer lines, grids, rings and waves, then change their spacing and rotation to draw optical interference art.

Inside the instrument

How to use MoiréField

  1. 01Choose two pattern layers and select a pattern for each.
  2. 02Adjust spacing, angle and position; use Lab warps or Auto Movement to animate their relationship.
  3. 03Choose a canvas size, including vertical 9:16, then export a still or record WebM in the browser.
